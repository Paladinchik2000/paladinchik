## Project Note

TireBuddy is a mobile tire service that drives to the customer instead of asking them to visit a shop, so the site is built around one job: turning a visitor into a booking without a phone call. The booking form sits on the landing page, posts to Formspree, and is guarded by reCAPTCHA, with Google Tag Manager and GA4 recording the conversion.

The rest is local-search work. Structured data marks the business up as an `AutoRepair` with a named service area, and the eleven cities it covers are stated in the page rather than left implied. Seasonal offers and the seniors discount are treated as real content with their own sections, which keeps the promotions indexable instead of hiding them in an image.

Built with React and Vite, styled with Tailwind, and spread across service, about, blog, FAQ, and contact pages.
